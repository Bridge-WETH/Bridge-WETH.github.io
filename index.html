How a WETH Bridge works

WETH is ETH wrapped as an ERC-20 so contracts can handle it like any token. A WETH Bridge moves that token between chains: you choose the amount, review the route and the amount that will arrive, then sign. You can also wrap and unwrap one-to-one between ETH and WETH on the same chain at any time.

WETH Bridge fees and time

The WETH Bridge costs the source-chain gas plus any protocol fee; wrapping or unwrapping on one chain is just a gas fee with no spread. Cross-chain transfers settle after source confirmations, usually minutes. The quote shows the amount received before you commit.

WETH Bridge: why DeFi uses WETH, not ETH

Native ETH is not an ERC-20, so many contracts work with WETH to treat it like other tokens in pools, lending and swaps. Bridging WETH keeps that compatibility on the destination chain, where it can be unwrapped back to ETH when you need native gas.

Is a WETH Bridge safe to use?

A self-custody route keeps your keys in your wallet, and wrapping is a one-to-one, reversible operation. Confirm the destination token's contract address, the amount received and the approval you grant, prefer a limited approval, and treat any seed-phrase request as fraud.

WETH Bridge vs wrapping on one chain

Wrapping converts ETH to WETH on the same chain one-to-one; bridging moves WETH to another chain. If you only need an ERC-20 locally, wrap; if you need it elsewhere, bridge. Judge any cross-chain route by the net amount received after gas and fees.
What is WETH?
WETH is Wrapped Ether, the ERC-20 form of ETH that contracts can handle like any token. It is backed one-to-one by ETH and reversible.
Do I need ETH to use a WETH Bridge?
Yes. You pay gas in ETH to wrap, bridge or unwrap, and you need a little native gas on the destination chain.
Can I turn WETH back into ETH?
Yes. Unwrapping converts WETH back to native ETH one-to-one on the same chain, for only a gas fee.
Do I need an account?
No. You connect a self-custody wallet and act on-chain, with no sign-up and no custody.
What happens if my transfer fails?
A failed transaction reverts and you keep your assets, though the gas for the attempt is spent.
