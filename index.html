What is it

What a WETH bridge does

A WETH bridge moves Wrapped Ether, the ERC-20 token that is worth 1:1 with ETH, from one network to another, for example from Ethereum to Arbitrum, Optimism, Base or Polygon. You connect your own wallet, choose the source and destination chain, and sign the transfer yourself.

What actually crosses

Many bridges are built around native ETH, so the route may unwrap WETH on the way in and deliver either ETH or WETH on the other side. The quote shows what you will receive on the destination chain before you sign.

Polygon is a special case

On Polygon PoS, bridged ETH arrives as WETH, because ETH is not the native coin there. On Arbitrum, Optimism and Base, ETH is the gas token, so routes often deliver native ETH.

WETH vs ETH

Bridging WETH vs bridging native ETH

The token you start with changes what you sign and what you get back. The destination asset also depends on the route and the chain.

Which to choose

If your funds are already WETH, bridge them as WETH and check the destination asset in the quote. You can wrap or unwrap 1:1 later on either side.

How to bridge

How to bridge WETH

Route and quote

The interface compares available routes for your pair of chains and shows the amount you will receive, the destination asset, the estimated time and the fees. Nothing is signed until you confirm.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and select the source chain, the destination chain and WETH as the token.
  2. Enter an amount and review the route, the asset you will receive and the estimated gas.
  3. Approve WETH once if this is your first bridge of it from this wallet on that chain.
  4. Sign the bridge transaction and wait for the transfer to be confirmed on both chains.
  5. Switch your wallet to the destination chain to see the ETH or WETH balance.

Leaving an L2 for Ethereum through the native rollup route is slower than going in, see the fees section.

Fees & gas

What bridging WETH costs

Gas on each chain

You pay gas in the native token of the chain you sign on, which is ETH on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism and Base, and POL on Polygon. See how gas works. Holding WETH does not pay for gas, so keep a small native balance on the chain you are leaving, and on the destination to move funds afterwards.

Bridge fee

Some routes add a protocol or liquidity fee, which is included in the quoted amount you receive. The fee and the route can differ between providers.

Withdrawal time

Native withdrawals from an optimistic rollup such as Arbitrum or Optimism back to Ethereum go through a challenge window of about 7 days. Fast bridges are quicker because a liquidity provider fronts the funds, and they charge a fee for it.

Security

Staying safe when you bridge

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. The interface routes and quotes, and it never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Check the source chain, destination chain and destination address in your wallet prompt.
  • Verify the WETH contract address on each chain, since fake and lookalike tokens exist.
  • Approve only what you need, and revoke old ERC-20 allowances you no longer use.
  • Bridge contracts can contain bugs and some routes rely on external validators or liquidity providers, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

WETH Bridge FAQ

What is a WETH bridge?

A WETH bridge moves Wrapped Ether from one network to another, such as Ethereum to Base or Arbitrum. Depending on the route, you may receive ETH or WETH on the destination chain, and the quote shows which.

Will I receive ETH or WETH on the other chain?

It depends on the route and the chain. On Arbitrum, Optimism and Base routes often deliver native ETH, while on Polygon PoS bridged ETH arrives as WETH. Check the destination asset in the quote before you sign.

Do I need to approve WETH before bridging?

Yes, if you bridge WETH as an ERC-20 token, the bridge contract needs a one-time allowance. Native ETH needs no approval. The approval is a separate transaction that costs gas on the source chain.

Which token pays gas when I bridge?

Gas is paid in the native token of the chain you are signing on, not in WETH. That is ETH on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism and Base, and POL on Polygon, so keep a small native balance on each chain you use.

How long does it take to bridge WETH back to Ethereum?

A native withdrawal from an optimistic rollup takes about 7 days because of the challenge window. Fast bridges are quicker for a fee, since liquidity providers front the funds on Ethereum.

Can I convert between WETH and ETH after bridging?

Yes. Wrapping and unwrapping are fixed 1:1 conversions that cost only gas, with no slippage. You can do it on the destination chain once your funds arrive.
